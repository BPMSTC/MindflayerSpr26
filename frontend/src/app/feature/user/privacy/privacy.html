Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Mindflayer Games ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you play Catmon and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details you provide when you sign up (such as email address).
  • Gameplay progress, including which CatMon you've encountered and the choices you've made.
  • Basic device and browser information used to keep the game running smoothly.

How We Use Your Information

  • To save your adventure progress and personalize your experience.
  • To improve the game, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • To respond to support questions you send through the Contact page.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers that help us run the game, and only as needed to operate it.

Your Choices

You can request access to or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us at support@mindflayer.games.

Children's Privacy

Catmon is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
