About the Adventure

MindFlayer Games was established in 2026 to create a this project for our Collaborative Application Developement class at Mid-State Technical School. We wanted to create a fun, interactive experience that combines storytelling with user choice, allowing players to shape their own unique adventure. We built this experience to capture the magic of classic choose-your-own-adventure stories while bringing them into a modern, interactive format.
