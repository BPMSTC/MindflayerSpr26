Privacy Policy

MindFlayer Games respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Catmon, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our site you agree to the practices described below.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

What We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account we store the email address and username you provide so we can save your progress and let you log back in. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and never in plain text.

Gameplay Data

We save the choices you make in each adventure, the Catmon you unlock, and your overall progress so you can pick up where you left off. This data is tied to your account and is never sold to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

We use a small number of cookies to keep you signed in and to understand which stories players enjoy most. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings without losing access to the site.

Your Choices

Access & Deletion

You may request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing privacy@mindflayergames.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Catmon is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.
