GAMEPLAY

What is CatMon?

CatMon is a text-based choose your own adventure game with monster-taming elements where your decisions shape your journey.

What will you choose?

Pick your CatMon, pick a path, and shape your destiny in this immersive text-based adventure! You can decide on what your character will do. Will you explore the world, battle other trainers, or focus on collecting and training your CatMon? The choice is yours!

What will you discover?

Embark on a quest to unravel the mysteries of the CatMon world, collecting a diverse array of CatMon to become the ultimate CatMon master. Along the way, you'll encounter various challenges and make choices that will determine your fate.
