Our Story

Three generations of passion, tradition, and innovation

Founded in 1987 by master baker Maria Gonzalez, Hearth & Crust began as a small neighborhood bakery with big dreams. Today, her granddaughter Elena continues the family tradition while bringing modern touches to time-honored recipes.

From our signature sourdough starter that's nearly 40 years old to our innovative seasonal offerings, we bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and contemporary tastes.
