Simple Sourdough Starter

⏱️ 7 Days 👨‍🍳 Beginner

Creating your own sourdough starter is easier than you think! All you need is flour, water, and patience. This living culture will become your baking companion for years to come.

Pro Tip:

Feed your starter at the same time every day and keep it in a warm spot (around 75°F). Look for bubbles and a pleasant, tangy aroma – these are signs of a healthy, active starter.

🥐

Spelt Croissants

⏱️ 2 Days 👨‍🍳 Advanced

Master the art of lamination with this classic recipe. While time-intensive, the result is worth every fold – impossibly flaky, buttery croissants that rival any French bakery.

Pro Tip:

Keep everything cold! Work quickly during lamination and return the dough to the fridge if the butter starts to soften. The key to flaky layers is maintaining distinct butter and dough.

Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookies

⏱️ 45 Minutes 👨‍🍳 Easy

Elevate the classic chocolate chip cookie. This simple technique adds deep, nutty flavors and creates cookies with crispy edges and chewy centers.

Pro Tip:

Chill the dough for at least 2 hours before baking. This allows the flour to fully hydrate and prevents spreading. For extra flavor, use a mix of milk and dark chocolate chunks.

🍞

Easy No-Knead Bread

⏱️ 18 Hours 👨‍🍳 Beginner

This revolutionary method requires minimal effort for maximum reward. Just mix flour, water, salt, and a tiny bit of yeast, then let time do the work to develop flavor and structure.

Pro Tip:

Bake in a preheated Dutch oven with the lid on for the first 30 minutes. This traps steam and creates that coveted crispy, deeply browned crust.

Classic Cupcakes

⏱️ 1 Hour 👨‍🍳 Easy

Light and fluffy cupcakes. Perfect for any occasion and endlessly customizable with different frostings and decorations.

Pro Tip:

Room temperature ingredients are crucial! Let eggs and butter sit out for 30 minutes before starting. This ensures proper emulsification and a tender crumb.

🥧

All-Butter Country Pie Crust

⏱️ 3 Hours 👨‍🍳 Intermediate

A flaky, tender pie crust is the foundation of great pies. This recipe uses only butter (no shortening) for superior flavor and texture. Master this and you'll never buy store-bought again.

Pro Tip:

Work quickly and handle the dough as little as possible. Visible butter pieces are good – they create steam pockets during baking that result in flakiness. Always chill before rolling.
